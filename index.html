INFECTION MODELS · PAPER 16

Incidence identifies unknown infection stages and weights

A complete noiseless incidence germ and known population determine the unknown finite stage number, both rates, normalized weights and all initial states within the positive single-chain model. The models have positive rates, weights and initial states, w₁ = 1, a common progression rate and the same physical time.

Checked by: A prose proof, supplemented by five bounded exact checks and staged AI review. The component reviews were cumulative; the integrated manuscript also received a fresh AI reading

MATHEMATICAL PHYSICS · PAPER 14

An interval certificate for a relativistic elastic ball beyond the Buchdahl compactness value

For one fixed material and central value δc = 8, interval certificates establish a regular finite elastic ball with 0.91089951 < 2M/R < 0.91180552, above 8/9; physical material viability and stability are outside the claim.

Checked by: Two interval certificates, with 465 and 1592 tubes, replayed through a shared integer interval engine

COMPARTMENTAL MODELS · PAPER 17

A leak-position condition for singular hyperplanes

Under the source article’s generic local identifiability assumptions, the proposed hyperplane inclusion holds for every n ≥ 3 when the leak is strictly before the output. A positive three-compartment counterexample rules out the universal inclusion at the output endpoint.

Checked by: A prose proof using a polynomial kernel identity and an equal-column argument, supplemented by three exact finite-check programs and staged AI review with advance hints. A later manuscript AI review led to two expository corrections.
