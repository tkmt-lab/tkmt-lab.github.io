Privacy policy for Spira Research Drive

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · tkmt-lab

What the app is

Spira Research Drive is an internal tool of tkmt-lab. Its only user is the lab's owner, Tatsuhiko Takimoto. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it does with Google data

  • It asks for one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With this permission it can only see and change files that it created itself in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It saves the lab's own computation results (for example, proof files from computer-assisted mathematics) into a folder in the owner's Drive, and reads back their size and checksum.
  • It does not read, list or change any other file in the Drive, and it does not access email, contacts, calendars or other Google data.

Storage and sharing

  • The authorization is stored only on the owner's own computer, with access limited to the owner's account. It is never published or sent to third parties.
  • No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Questions: research@tatsuhikotakimoto.com
