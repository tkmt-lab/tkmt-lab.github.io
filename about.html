Process, acknowledgements and contact

I publish results with documented checks. These packages have not undergone human peer review; publication here does not establish novelty.

How the work is checked

Each release keeps different kinds of evidence apart, and says which applies to each claim:

Formal proofChecked by the Lean 4 kernel. Separately, the Comparator tool checks that the proof proves exactly a statement written in advance by a different agent. Anyone can re-run the build; each repository's GitHub Actions workflow rebuilds the main modules and checks their axioms on every push. Any step from the paper to the formal statement that is not in Lean is said so in the repository.
Independent derivationA second proof written by a separate team that did not see the first proof or its code. This does not by itself mean that the statement, expected result or mathematical preparation was withheld. Each work records what was shared; a separate implementation and an AI reading are different checks.
AI reviewProse read and recalculated by an AI reviewer.
Finite computationExhaustive or sampled checks, with their exact ranges.

The main theorems of Papers 1, 2 and 6 are also written as Lean statements, without proofs, in a separate statement collection (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23594152). Each statement links to its proof at a fixed commit; building the collection does not check the proofs.

Each repository records the checks, unresolved points and corrections for its claims.

All results, their claims, kinds of evidence and commands to re-check them are listed in one machine-readable file: catalog of results (catalog.json).

How each kind of check is done, what it establishes and what it leaves open, as a procedure others can reuse: how the work is checked.

How this research is done

The research is carried out with AI systems (OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude Code) working as separate teams for exploration, independent verification, literature search and formalization. I, Tatsuhiko Takimoto, direct the work, make the decisions, and am responsible for what is released, its provenance and the stated limits of verification. AI systems are not listed as authors.
